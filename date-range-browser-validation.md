# Date-Range Browser Validation

Validated product 90003 on the live dashboard preview.

The shared product route now defaults to **All stored history** and displayed **88 stored price records**, spanning the database range from January 7, 2026 through August 17, 2026. The chart visibly rendered the longer historical curve instead of only the most recent record.

Selecting the **30d** preset changed the heading to **Price History (Last 30 days)** and displayed **1 stored price record in this range**, matching the user-observed recent-history behavior while preserving access to older records through All, 90d, 1 year, and Custom.
